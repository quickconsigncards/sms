<h1>Quick Consign Priority Listing &mdash; SMS Service</h1>

<p>Quick Consign offers an SMS-based Priority Listing service to existing consignors. Active Quick Consign consignors can text <strong>866-989-7874</strong> to request expedited eBay listing service for their consigned cards.</p>

<h2>Service Description</h2>
<p>By texting our Priority Listing number, consignors request an expedited eBay listing for cards already in our consignment inventory. Quick Consign responds with automated confirmations, status updates, photo requirements, and operational notices related to the request. A $5 service fee applies and is deducted from the consignor's next weekly payout.</p>

<h2>SMS Terms</h2>
<p>By texting <strong>866-989-7874</strong>, you consent to receive automated SMS and MMS messages from Quick Consign related to your Priority Listing request, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>Confirmation of receipt</li>
<li>Status updates</li>
<li>Operational notices (photo issues, missing information, daily limits)</li>
</ul>
<p>Message frequency varies based on your activity. Message and data rates may apply.</p>

<p>Reply <strong>STOP</strong> to opt out of Priority Listing messages at any time.<br>
Reply <strong>HELP</strong> for support.</p>

<div class="contact">
For questions or assistance, contact: <strong>support@quickconsigncards.com</strong>
</div>

<h2>Privacy</h2>
<p>Quick Consign does not share your phone number or message content with third parties for marketing purposes. SMS data is used solely to deliver the Priority Listing service.</p>

<h2>Eligibility</h2>
<p>This service is available to active Quick Consign consignors with established accounts. New customers should contact us through standard channels to set up consignment service before using Priority Listing.</p>
